   Defined in advance:
   - NAMES (the 181 tensor names), SHAPES (name -> shape) and VOCAB (/model/vocab.json: codes, normalisation, bin edges
     and centres, sizes). The weight values themselves are not available to you.
   - You edit the model with operations. They are applied in order to the original weights whenever the forward tool runs
     the edited model, and at the evaluation:
     scale(name, c): multiply a whole tensor by the number c;
     rank1(name, vector, alpha, side="out"): a rank-1 update along the unit direction v of the vector. With side "out" (v
     has shape[0] entries), W <- W + alpha * v (v^T W): the tensor's output component along v is scaled by 1 + alpha.
     With side "in" (2-D tensors, v has shape[1] entries), W <- W + alpha * (W v) v^T. For a 1-D tensor,
     w <- w + alpha * v (v . w);
     ops() lists the operations, undo() removes the last one, and clear_ops() removes them all.
     Directions can come from anything you compute: the records, or the states the forward tool returns.
   Model files: /model/ARCHITECTURE.md (how the model computes; read it first), /model/tensors.json and /model/vocab.json.
