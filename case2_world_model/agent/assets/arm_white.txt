   Defined in advance:
   - NAMES (the 181 tensor names), SHAPES (name -> shape) and VOCAB (/model/vocab.json: codes, normalisation, bin edges
     and centres, sizes);
   - load(name): your current edit of a tensor if there is one, else the original, as float32; load_original(name);
   - save_edit(name, tensor): replace a tensor by an edited version of the same shape; drop_edit(name) goes back to the
     original; edited() lists the tensors you have edited.
   Model files: /model/ARCHITECTURE.md (how the model computes; read it first), /model/model.safetensors (the weights),
   /model/tensors.json and /model/vocab.json.
